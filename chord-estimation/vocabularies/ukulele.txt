# The ukulele vocabulary: chord types with at most 4 notes, written on C.
# A ukulele has four strings, so it can sound at most four different notes.
# [ORIGINAL] list, 2026-11-24, as follows:
#   - every type in lv-chordia's `submission` dictionary with at most 4 notes,
#     left without its slash bass (the bass is not scored: chord_decoder --no-bass);
#   - plus maj6, min6, minmaj7, add9 and minor add9, which have 4 notes and
#     shapes in chords-db's ukulele list (suffixes 6, m6, mmaj7, add9, madd9).
# Left out: 9, maj9, min9 (5 notes), 11 (6 notes), 13 (7 notes).
# Note counts are checked by tests/chord-estimation/test_chord_decoder.py with
# common/harte.py, and expressibility by lv-chordia's heads with chord_indices.
# The 6th and add-9 chords use the 13th and 9th heads: decode WITHOUT
# --no-extended, or they tie with plain major and minor.
